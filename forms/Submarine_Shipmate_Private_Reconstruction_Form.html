Boat-Neutral Reconstruction Guidance

For each remembered event, identify the boat, approximate date, compartment, equipment/system, task, material nickname/product if remembered, exposure route, duration/frequency, PPE, immediate symptoms, later symptoms, witnesses, and records that might verify it.

Evidence labels: Firsthand recollection; boat-specific documentation; class/period Navy documentation; SDS/MSDS/toxicology; research candidate; medical diagnosis. Keep these separate.

Do not assume: a the specific ship system was installed on another boat; a Navy-authorized chemical was actually used by a particular sailor; an SDS symptom proves exposure; or a symptom match establishes causation.
