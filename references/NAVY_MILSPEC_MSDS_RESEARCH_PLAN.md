# Department of the Navy MILSPEC & Industry MSDS/SDS Research Plan

**Public Reference — Information Only**

This register supports systematic reconstruction of materials associated with submarine and submarine-tender tasks. It does not establish that a listed material was aboard a particular vessel, that an individual encountered it, or that it caused any medical condition.

## Evidence chain

1. Navy/DoD specification or standard
2. exact period revision and distribution status
3. Navy/submarine/tender application evidence
4. QPL/QPD, NSN/NIIN, or other product identity evidence
5. manufacturer/trade product
6. period MSDS where available
7. modern SDS for context where period MSDS is unavailable

A military specification is **not** an MSDS/SDS. A modern qualified product or SDS does **not** prove that the same product or formulation was supplied in the 1980s.

## Priority workstreams

- atmosphere control and air-compressor materials
- hydraulic fluids
- lubricating oils and greases
- refrigeration and air-conditioning materials
- solvents, degreasers and cleaning compounds
- oxygen-system cleaning materials
- graphite/silicone compounds, sealants and packing
- paints/coatings and surface preparation
- diesel/fuel and bilge-maintenance materials
- welding, cutting and tender-shop consumables
- electrical/battery materials
- insulation/asbestos/packing materials
- sanitary-system cleaners/descalers

## Registers

- `navy-period-material-spec-discovery.csv` — official-specification discovery and acquisition status.
- `industry-msds-sds-discovery.csv` — manufacturer/product/MSDS/SDS leads.

“ACQUIRE” means the official record currently supports a public Statement-A period component suitable for controlled acquisition. “VERIFY” means the exact period component or distribution status still needs to be established before preservation.

## Batch 2 expansion

The second discovery pass adds submarine camouflage coatings, NAVSEA high-temperature antiseize, graphitic valve packing, shipboard coating systems, electric-motor bearing grease, corrosion-preventive compounds, and period NAVSEA welding consumables.

A-A-50433 is deliberately treated as **post-period context** for 1985–1988 research: its base commercial item description dates to 1989. Later Navy guidance documents the fleet changeover from MIL-PRF-24139 to A-A-50433 on submarines, but that later replacement must not be projected backward without period evidence.

`period-spec-acquisition-batch-2.csv` contains Statement-A document components whose exact official record/revision/date have been established sufficiently for controlled acquisition with the existing QuickSearch resolver. It is a research batch list; it does not itself prove ship-specific use.

## PMS / MRC / FOIA evidence stream

See `submarine-pms-mrc-foia-discovery.csv` and `SUBMARINE_PMS_MRC_FOIA_REQUEST.md`.

PMS/MRC records are especially valuable because an MRC can identify the exact task, safety precautions, tools, parts, materials, and test equipment. Treat an MRC as task/material evidence, not as proof that a particular sailor performed the task. Historical revision dates remain essential.
