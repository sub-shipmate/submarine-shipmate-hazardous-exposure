# Batch 4 acquisition

This batch adds two exact DLA Statement-A targets for the corrosion-preventive compound specification that governed during the 1985–1988 research period:

- MIL-C-16173 Revision D — 20 October 1966
- MIL-C-16173 Revision D Amendment 2 — 19 November 1968

Both are listed by the official DLA record under MIL-PRF-16173 with Distribution Statement A.

Run the normal period-spec acquisition script. Do not add the PDFs to the manifest until acquisition succeeds and their hashes are verified.

The research registers also add qualified-product, NSN, and MSDS/product-identity leads for MIL-C-16173, MIL-L-15719, MIL-L-24478, and MIL-P-24548.
