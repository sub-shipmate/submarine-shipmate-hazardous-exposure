# Controlled-document link policy

**Public Reference — Information Only**

For research-relevant documents marked Distribution Statement C, export-controlled, NOFORN, CUI, or otherwise restricted:

1. retain the public record or publicly discoverable PDF URL as research metadata;
2. record the exact restriction/distribution statement when established;
3. mark `local_pdf_archived = NO`;
4. do not mirror or redistribute the restricted PDF through this repository;
5. retain historical revision/date information when useful for a FOIA or reading-room request; and
6. assess each revision independently because distribution markings can change over time.

This distinguishes **discoverability** from **redistribution authorization**.
