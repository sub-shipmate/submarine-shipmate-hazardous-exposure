# Targeted FOIA request — historical submarine PMS/MRC material

**Public Reference — Information Only**

This is a research template. It requests records, not answers to questions, and asks only for unclassified/releasable material.

## Suggested first request scope

Records sought: electronic copies of the unclassified/releasable Maintenance Index Pages (MIPs), Maintenance Requirement Cards (MRCs), locator cards, Standard PMS Material Identification Guide (SPMIG) entries, and associated material/tool lists in effect during 1 January 1985 through 31 December 1988 for U.S. Navy nuclear attack submarines, with priority on SSN-637/SSN-594-era boats and the following atmosphere-control identifiers or their predecessor/successor numbers:

- MIP 5151/001 — atmosphere-control / LiOH or CO2-absorbent maintenance lineage.
- MIP 5154/009 — oxygen candle furnace / chlorate candle maintenance lineage.
- Any MIPs/MRCs for CO2 scrubber, CO/H2 burner, oxygen generation/candle equipment, atmosphere-control fans/filters, high-pressure air compressors, refrigeration/A-C equipment, hydraulic systems, diesel generator, sanitary systems, and associated cleaning/lubrication tasks.
- Associated SPMIG entries identifying chemicals, cleaners, lubricants, greases, solvents, sealants, packing, coatings, refrigerants, or other consumable materials required by those MRCs.

Please provide records electronically. If a responsive record contains exempt material, please release all reasonably segregable non-exempt portions. If exact 1985–1988 revisions are no longer retained, please provide the nearest retained predecessor/successor revision and any revision-history/index record sufficient to identify the version in force during that period.

This request does not seek classified, NOFORN, CUI, nuclear-propulsion technical information, tactical/operational information, security-sensitive system vulnerabilities, or currently sensitive technical parameters. Redacted/segregated copies are acceptable.

Please advise before incurring fees above $25.

## Why these identifiers are reasonably specific

Public Navy sources identify MIP 5151/001 and MIP 5154/009 as submarine atmosphere-control PMS references. NAVSEA's public PMS document schema recognizes an unclassified MRC document class. A NASA/Navy maintenance study also indexes historical SSN-688-class maintenance-plan material and URO MRC records, demonstrating that specific submarine maintenance records can be described by class, MIP/MRC identifier, and revision.
